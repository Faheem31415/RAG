# Brew Haven Café – Complete Handbook

## 1. About Brew Haven Café

Brew Haven Café is a modern coffee shop designed for coffee lovers, students, professionals, and people who enjoy a comfortable environment.

Our goal is to provide high-quality coffee, fresh bakery products, friendly customer service, and a relaxing atmosphere.

We use carefully selected coffee beans sourced from local and international coffee farms. Our baristas prepare every drink using professional coffee machines and freshly ground coffee.

## 6. Ordering Process

Customers can order food and beverages in three ways:

1. **Counter Ordering:** Customers can visit the counter and place an order directly.
2. **Online Ordering:** Customers can place orders through the café's website.
3. **Takeaway:** Customers can order and collect their food from the café.

Orders are prepared after payment confirmation.

The average preparation time is:
- Regular coffee: 5–7 minutes.
- Specialty coffee: 7–10 minutes.
- Bakery products: 2–5 minutes.
- Sandwiches and burgers: 10–15 minutes.

Preparation times may increase during busy hours.

## 9. Customer Policies

### Refund Policy

- Orders can be cancelled before preparation begins.
- Refunds are considered if the café delivers an incorrect or defective order.
- Refund requests should be reported within 30 minutes of receiving the order.
- Approved refunds are processed through the original payment method where possible.

### Outside Food Policy

Customers are generally not allowed to consume outside food inside the café.

Exceptions may be made for specific dietary or medical requirements with staff approval.

### Pet Policy

Pets are not permitted inside the indoor dining area. Customers should contact the café regarding any applicable accessibility or service-animal arrangements.

### Reservation Policy

Customers can reserve tables for groups of four or more.

Reservations should preferably be made at least two hours in advance.

## 10. Loyalty Program

Brew Haven Café offers a loyalty program called Brew Rewards.

Customers earn points for eligible purchases.

Reward rules:

- Every ₹100 spent earns 10 reward points.
- 100 points can be redeemed for a ₹50 discount.
- Customers must provide their registered mobile number to collect points.
- Reward points cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Special promotional offers may provide additional points.

## 11. Allergens and Dietary Information
